Organizing a large event takes a surprising amount of work, something I severely 
underestimated at first! While this project isn't very technical, it's something 
I'm super proud of as I put in a ton of time and effort in order to pull it off. 

### Key Accomplishments Include...
- Raised $8,000 via fundraising and ticketing to rent a formal boat cruise on 
Lake Michigan, cover guest transportation, and provide dinner and drinks.
- Managed guest RSVPs, payments, plus-one requests, and dietary concerns for over 150 people. 
- Held voting for venue selection, acted as point of contact towards venue provider, and 
secured mid-event photography services months in advance. 
- Had an awesome time with my friends on Lake Michigan!!!
